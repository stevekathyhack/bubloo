Bubloo 👶

A calm handoff app for parents, grandparents, and caregivers.

Most baby tracking apps focus on recording everything.

Bubloo focuses on helping the next caregiver quickly understand the baby's current state.

Whether you're handing off care to your spouse, a grandparent, or a babysitter, Bubloo provides a simple summary of what matters right now.

Why Bubloo?

Traditional baby tracking apps answer:

How many ounces did the baby drink today?
How many diapers were changed?
How many hours did the baby sleep?

Bubloo answers:

"If I'm taking over right now, what do I need to know?"

Because during sleep deprivation, nobody wants to scroll through dozens of logs trying to reconstruct the last six hours.

AI Handoff Summary

Generate a natural-language summary for the next caregiver.

Emma slept from 2:00 PM to 3:15 PM and had a bottle at 3:30 PM.

Design Principles
Calm Over Guilt

Many parenting apps create anxiety through:

Excessive notifications
Performance scores
Streaks
Pressure to log everything

Bubloo intentionally avoids these patterns.

Simple Over Perfect

We believe incomplete information that is easy to access is often more useful than perfect information buried in complicated dashboards.

Human Over Data

The goal is not collecting data.

The goal is helping people care for a child together.
